How it works

Installs in minutes. Runs in the agent you already use.

No new platform to learn. Skills land as SKILL.md folders your agent already understands.

1. Install

Download a kit zip, or copy one skill folder into your agent’s skills directory. One command for CLI agents; drag-and-drop for folder-based setups.

2. Connect

Fill in config.yaml once: inbox account, CRM, timezone, company voice, and which tools the skill may read. Examples ship with every kit.

3. Approve

Run on a schedule or on demand. Drafts appear in your agent chat or a designated folder. You approve the exact item — send, edit, or ignore.

config.example.yaml

Template
timezone: Asia/Riyadh
voice: senior, brief, no hype
inbox:
  provider: gmail
  label: needs-reply
crm:
  provider: your-crm
  stages: [lead, qualified, proposal]
approval:
  mode: exact-item
  never_auto_send: true

Skills run on your machine and your accounts. Easy By You never receives your inbox or CRM data.
