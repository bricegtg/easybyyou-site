Trust & safety

The rules our skills don’t break.

You’re putting an agent near email, CRM, and money. These four guarantees are non-negotiable.

Draft-only

Our skills prepare emails, messages, records, and documents. You send, post, sign, and pay. No skill ever gets a blanket “send anything” permission.

No invented numbers

Every figure carries its source and period. If the data isn’t there, the skill says “not available.” It never estimates revenue, headcount, or cash.

Quiet when idle

No “all clear” noise. A skill speaks up only when something needs you — a reply to draft, a deal going cold, a claim that fails review.

Your yes on the exact item

Approval covers one specific email, record, or change. Never a standing authorisation to act without you.

Data handling

Skills run inside your agent, on your accounts. Easy By You does not host your inbox, CRM, or financial data. We do not claim SOC 2, ISO, or GDPR certification we don’t hold. What we can say: designed so your data stays in your own accounts.

Higher-risk skills

Finance Snapshot is read-only. It never moves money or edits the ledger.

Paperwork Factory produces drafts for review by qualified counsel. Not legal advice. Never sends or signs.

Site Audit legal checks are a gap analysis, not advice.
